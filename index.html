01 / 04 · Frontier evals

Evals that predict production.

Custom benchmarks built from your real use cases — not leaderboard trivia. Capability elicitation, LLM-as-judge calibrated against expert humans, contamination checks, and a harness you keep.

  • Capability maps per checkpoint
  • Rubrics & judge calibration
  • Contamination & regression checks
02 / 04 · Red-teaming

We break it before your users do.

Adversarial testing by people who live inside these models. Jailbreak discovery, prompt-injection audits for agents, refusal calibration and reward-hacking hunts — every finding reproducible.

  • Jailbreak & policy bypass
  • Prompt injection · tool abuse
  • Over- & under-refusal
03 / 04 · Post-training data

Data that moves the loss curve.

Expert-written SFT demonstrations, preference pairs for RLHF and DPO, and reasoning traces — designed against the failures we found, and measured on the evals that matter.

  • SFT demonstrations
  • RLHF · DPO preference pairs
  • Reasoning traces & rubrics
04 / 04 · RL environments

Worlds for agents to learn in.

Verifiable tasks with graders that can't be gamed — tool-use, browser and workflow sandboxes where agents roll out, fail, and get better. Built for long-horizon reliability.

  • Verifiable tasks & graders
  • Tool-use & computer-use sandboxes
  • Long-horizon trajectories
